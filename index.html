AMPL BILL No. 1. An act relating to representation in law and policy. Introduced by students at six University of California campuses

Muslim students are underrepresented in belong in every courtroom, legislature, and policy room.

Digest

AMPL, the Association of Muslims in Politics and Law, is a student network that began at UC San Diego and now has chapters at six UC campuses. Members meet the people who work in law and policy, practice the skills, and find each other. Membership is free and open to all students.

Pick your campus to see how to join.

SEC. 1.Signature programs

Law and policy, studied alone practiced together.

(a)

Mocktails & Mock Trials

Our signature night: a mock trial, a mocktail bar, and a room of students who want to argue a case and meet each other.

(b)

Speaker panels

People working in government, the courts, and legal education talk with students about the paths they took.

(c)

Policy workshops

Skill-building sessions for students heading into law and policy, such as writing for policy change.

Policy, written by the people it affects.

(d)

Community nights

General meetings, movie nights, and socials. The part where a chapter becomes the people you call.
